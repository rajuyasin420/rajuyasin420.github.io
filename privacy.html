NOVEM privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026.

NOVEM is a personal automation tool with a single user: its owner, who runs it on their own computer. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

Google data it uses

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's own channel, reads and edits that channel's video details, and reads that channel's own analytics.
  • Gmail (read-only): reads sign-in code emails from Whop and Content Rewards in the owner's own inbox, so it can sign in to those services. It does not read, store or act on any other email.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and all data stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

NOVEM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting NOVEM's local token files removes everything it stored.
